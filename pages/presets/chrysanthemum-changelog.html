Changelog

Update history for the Chrysanthemum preset.

October 19, 2026 — v2.0.0
  • Reorganized with XML instead of markdown. Most of the character instructions now live in the Contract, with dialogue in Speaking Style and response formatting in Turn Start.
  • Reworked the character prompts: more emphasis on their own agendas, imperfect knowledge, and limited self-awareness. They shouldn't slip into helpful-assistant mode or revolve around {{user}}.
  • Tweaked relationships and emotional honesty. Being close doesn't mean being transparent, sex doesn't automatically mean love, and characters don't have to explain their own logic.
  • Revised dialogue instructions, including vocabulary that fits the setting and time period, code switching, and less polished banter. Character info is context, not a checklist to perform every turn.
  • Added Short, Average, Long, and Flexible length options. Pick ONE at a time; Average is on by default.
  • Replaced User Autonomy with Write for User. Leave it OFF to play your own persona; turn it ON if you want the model to write both sides.
  • Moved prose examples into optional Guidance, so you can turn those off to save tokens. Narration defaults to third person, past tense, with a tone suited to the POV character.
  • More direction to continue with new action, stop echoing the user's wording, and end on something that moves the scene along.
  • Added an optional Jailbreak, originally made for MiMo 2.5. Not sure how well it works on other models, so YMMV.
  • Uses regular setvar/getvar macros; no Experimental Macro Engine required. Kept v1.0.0 available below the main download if you prefer the old version.
